## Final Investment Decision: Venture Global

I selected **Venture Global** as the best company for investment.

### Why Venture Global was chosen
Venture Global has the strongest overall investment case among the three companies because it offers the best combination of:
- **Large-scale LNG growth exposure**
- **Visible project pipeline and expansion milestones**
- **Strategic positioning in a high-demand global market**
- **Meaningful long-term upside potential**

The research indicates that Venture Global is advancing major LNG projects, including **CP2**, with construction approval and financing milestones already achieved. That is important because it reduces uncertainty relative to a purely conceptual growth story and shows tangible progress toward expanding export capacity. Among the three names, Venture Global is the only one that clearly stands out as a **high-growth infrastructure platform** with a strong long-duration expansion runway.

Even though there are risks — especially execution, financing, regulatory, and LNG market volatility — the company’s scale and project momentum make it the most attractive choice for an investor seeking growth. The upside potential is higher than what is available from the other two companies.

### Why the other companies were not selected

#### Energy Transfer LP
Energy Transfer is a strong, diversified midstream company, but it was not selected because its most notable LNG growth catalyst has weakened. The suspension of the **Lake Charles LNG export facility** reduces the company’s optionality and removes a potential long-term growth driver.

Energy Transfer still has:
- Stable infrastructure cash flows
- A large asset base
- Attractive income-oriented characteristics

However, compared with Venture Global, it is less compelling as a growth investment. Its profile is more conservative and more focused on core midstream stability than on major expansion. That makes it a solid company, but not the best investment choice in this comparison.

#### HighPeak Energy
HighPeak Energy was not selected because it is primarily a **special-situation M&A name**, not a clean operating-company growth story. The company is exploring a sale after receiving acquisition interest, which means the investment case depends heavily on:
- Whether a transaction happens
- The timing of the deal
- The eventual acquisition premium
- Crude market conditions

That makes HighPeak speculative and event-driven. While it could produce upside if a sale closes, the outcome is uncertain and less dependable than Venture Global’s more visible LNG growth path. For that reason, it ranks behind Venture Global.

### Conclusion
**Venture Global is the best investment choice** because it has the clearest and strongest growth trajectory, the most compelling strategic position, and the highest long-term upside of the three companies. Energy Transfer is more stable but less growth-oriented, and HighPeak Energy is more speculative and dependent on takeover outcomes.

**Recommendation: Venture Global**
